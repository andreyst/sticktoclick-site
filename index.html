Practice that answers back

A metronome tells you the time. StickToClick tells you what you actually played.

Real notation

Your charts, engraved properly — import MusicXML from MuseScore, Guitar Pro or Sibelius. Repeats can be shown written out, exactly as they play.

Timing you can interpret

Correct, early, late, missed, wrong drum — with Tight ±25 ms, Normal ±40 ms and Loose ±60 ms windows. See why those numbers matter.

Built for e-kits

Any MIDI kit. Pad test, per-kit mapping, and latency calibration so the scoring clock matches what you hear.

Focused loops

Pick bars 12–15, set repetitions, ramp the tempo. The count-in handles pickup bars like a drummer would.

Streaks & live stats

An optional overlay counts your verdicts and streaks as you play — and drops the dots on the score in real time.

Takes that teach

Every take is saved: replay what you played against the reference, loop the rough spots, watch accuracy climb over time.

Timing feedback, grounded in research

There is no magic millisecond that separates good from bad. StickToClick turns research on synchronisation, perception and groove into practical training levels, then calibrates out the fixed latency of your kit.

Which drum kits work?

Any electronic kit with MIDI out — USB or through an interface. Roland TD-series mapping is built in; everything else maps in a minute.

Where do charts come from?

MusicXML — the export every notation app has: MuseScore (free), Guitar Pro, Sibelius, Dorico.
