Version 1.0

July 17, 2026
  • The first release. Import a MusicXML drum chart, see it beautifully engraved, play it against the click on your e-kit — and watch every hit get judged on the notation: correct, early, late, missed, extra.
  • Two built-in courses to learn from scratch: Note Durations and Drum Notation.
  • Backing tracks with automatic tempo detection, so you can practice inside the real recording.
  • Practice history with take-by-take results, timing statistics, and replay.
  • Optional, anonymous usage analytics — counts and settings only, never titles, file names, or anything personal — to help us see which features matter. One switch in Settings → Privacy turns it off.
